# Handoff Policy

- Documents flagged with **high** severity issues, duplicate invoices, or bank detail changes are sent to a human reviewer.
- The reviewer receives the redacted PDF, extracted data, issues list, and a suggested reply.
- Reviewers can approve, modify, or reject the draft.
- All reviewer actions are logged in the `metrics` collection.
